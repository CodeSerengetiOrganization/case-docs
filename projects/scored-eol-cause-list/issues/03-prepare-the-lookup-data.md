# Prepare the lookup data

The agent looks up solved-case reports and maintenance logs. The two short documents in "Learn the model call" are practice. They are not this set.

1. Collect the solved-case reports and the maintenance logs.
2. Pick one solved case. Write down its true cause. Keep that cause out of the lookup set.
3. Leave the other cases and the logs in the lookup set.

Close this issue when the held-out case and the lookup set both exist.
